Six pairings.

Each calm face from the last round, now with a partner. In every pair the anchor does the reading and the companion does the talking, which is the only way a pairing stays restful.

DM Serif Display + DM Sans

Recommended. One family, two voices.

Both faces come from the same superfamily, so the proportions already agree and the pairing cannot clash. The serif brings a letter-from-a-friend warmth to the headline while DM Sans keeps every number plain.

If you want the pairing to disappear

The first and fifth are the safest, for opposite reasons. DM Serif Display and DM Sans share a designer and a skeleton, so they agree by construction. Figtree and Hanken Grotesk are simply close cousins. Everything in between trades a little calm for a little character, which is a fair trade as long as you make it on purpose.
