Fair questions.

Do we both get our own login?

Yes. One subscription, two logins. You each connect your own banks and see the same shared picture.

What if we keep some money separate?

Most couples do. You share accounts one at a time, and anything marked private never shows up for your partner.

What if one of us hates money stuff?

That's fine. One of you can fuss over the details while the other just reads the five-minute Sunday summary. Same picture, either way.

What happens if we split up?

Either of you can unlink your accounts at any time and take your data with you. Ask us to delete something and we delete it.

Can TwoFold move our money?

No. Connections are read-only. We can show you what's happening, but we can't touch it.
