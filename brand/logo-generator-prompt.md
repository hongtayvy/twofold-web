# Logo generator prompt

Paste the block below into an AI logo generator. Values verified against
`landing-concepts/twofold-coming-soon.html`; the overlap colour was sampled from a
rendered multiply blend rather than estimated.

---

Design a logo for **TwoFold**, a personal finance app for couples who manage money together.

**What it does:** puts both partners' bank accounts into one shared view, turning the monthly money argument into a five-minute check-in on Sunday.

**Tone:** calm, warm, plain-spoken. Trustworthy without being corporate. It handles real bank data so it must feel safe, but it is built for two people at a kitchen table, not for a bank boardroom. Avoid neon, gradients, glossy 3D, generic fintech blue, swooshes, shields, dollar signs, coins, piggy banks and handshakes.

**The current mark, as a starting point:** two equal circles side by side, overlapping. Each circle is 28 units across and their centres sit 12 units apart, about 43% of one diameter, so they intersect in a vertical lens roughly 16 units wide. The left circle is warm apricot, the right is soft jade, and where they cross the two colours multiply into a muted olive. The meaning is literal: one circle per partner, and the overlap is the money they share.

**Exact palette. Use these values, do not substitute:**
- Apricot, partner one: `#E3A377`
- Jade, partner two: `#7CBC9E`
- Overlap where the two meet: `#6E784A`
- Primary action jade: `#3B8168`
- Background paper: `#FAFAF7`
- Ink and text: `#18211C`

Two hue families only, warm apricot and cool jade. Never introduce a third.

**Wordmark, if included:** the name is "TwoFold", one word, capital T and capital F. Set it in a warm high-contrast serif such as DM Serif Display, Georgia-adjacent rather than Times. Any supporting text is DM Sans.

**Technical requirements:**
- Legible at 20x20px as a browser favicon
- Works reversed on a near-black `#18211C` background
- Has a single-colour version that survives with no colour at all
- Simple geometry only: no illustration, no gradients, no drop shadows

**Important constraint:** two equal overlapping circles in two colours is the Mastercard construction, which is disqualifying for a finance brand. Keep the idea of two things sharing an overlap, but change the geometry so it cannot be mistaken for Mastercard. Directions worth exploring: one circle divided into two halves; two shapes that interlock rather than overlap; two paths converging on a single point; a form beside the same form at twice the size; two panels hinged at a centre crease.

## Short version

For generators with a character limit:

> Logo for TwoFold, a calm personal finance app for couples. Two shapes that share an overlap, one per partner, the intersection representing shared money. Warm apricot `#E3A377` and soft jade `#7CBC9E`, meeting in olive `#6E784A`, on paper `#FAFAF7`. Two hues only. Simple geometry, no gradients or illustration. Must read at 20px and reverse on dark. Do not use two equal overlapping circles, that is Mastercard.

## A caveat

AI logo generators produce output that is hard to protect: it is often derivative of
training data, and in several jurisdictions purely machine-generated work is not
reliably copyrightable. Treat anything it returns as a sketch to hand a designer, and
run a trademark clearance check before it goes on anything real.
