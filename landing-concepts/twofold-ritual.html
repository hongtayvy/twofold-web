Fair questions.

What if one of us hates money stuff?

Then that person reads the weekly recap and answers one question. That is the entire job. The other one can go as deep as they like.

We keep our money mostly separate. Still useful?

Yes, and it is the common case. Share the joint account and the bills, keep the rest private. The shared view only ever holds what you both put in it.

Do we both get our own login?

One subscription, two logins. You each connect your own banks, and neither of you needs the other's password for anything.

What happens if we split up?

Either of you can unlink and leave at any time, with your own data. We will not hold one partner's history hostage to the other's account.

Can TwoFold move our money?

No. Connections are read-only, so we can show you what happened and nothing more.

That is two check-ins, free. If it is not for you, cancel before day 14 and you pay nothing.
