DM Sans

Recommended. The quiet one.

Low contrast, generous curves, nothing showing off. It is the closest thing to a typeface with no opinion, which is why so many finance products land on it. Calm by subtraction.

Lexend

Engineered to be easy

Drawn specifically to reduce reading effort, with wide apertures and loose spacing. Of the six this is the one that is calm on purpose rather than by taste, which is a real argument when the subject is money and the reader is tense.

Nunito Sans

The softest here

Rounded terminals throughout, the gentlest of the six. Worth seeing because it is unmistakably relaxed, and worth watching because that same softness can read as less serious next to a number with a dollar sign on it.

Why one family instead of two

Each specimen uses a single typeface for both headline and body, which is a change from the current page. Pairing two families adds tension, and tension is the opposite of what you asked for. A single family across a range of weights is quieter, loads faster, and is much harder to get wrong.
