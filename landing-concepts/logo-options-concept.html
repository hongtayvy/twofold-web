What twofold actually means.

Two ideas live in the word. Something becoming twice itself, and two contributions adding up to one result. Every mark below is one or the other. No digits, no paper.

Two, adding up to one

Converge

Recommended

Two paths starting apart and arriving at the same point. It is the clearest statement of the product: two people, separate incomes, one destination. It also reads upward, which quietly does the growth job at the same time.

Arch

One structure made of two halves, where removing either one collapses it. An arch is the oldest visual argument for mutual support, and it carries shelter and home without drawing a house.

Interlock

One rounded form split by a soft S, into two pieces that are the same piece turned around. Equal partners, fitted rather than merged. The most product-like of the six and the calmest at small sizes.

Cycle

Two arcs completing one ring, each the other rotated. It suits the weekly rhythm of the product: not a finish line, a loop you both keep going. Reads as continuity rather than achievement.

Becoming twice itself

Double

A form, and the same form at exactly twice the size, drawn to the measurement rather than by eye. Twofold as multiplication. The inner shape is what you have, the outer is where it is going.

TwoFold 20px on dark

Bloom

Two lines growing out of one shared root. The warmest option, and the only one that says the money came from somewhere together rather than being split apart. Softest at very small sizes.

One thing to be careful about

Marks about growth get read as promises. An upward line next to a finance brand can imply a return you are not offering, which is a claim regulators care about. Converge and Bloom both point up. If that worries you, Interlock and Arch say partnership without saying performance.
